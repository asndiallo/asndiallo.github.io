## Before

The salon's Google Business Profile had never been claimed. That meant the owner couldn't control what Google showed about her business: her hours, her address, or her photos.

## What I did

- **Claimed the Google Business Profile** for the salon, so the owner controls it. Then I corrected the hours, confirmed the address, and added real photos of her work.
- **Built [isabraiding.com](https://isabraiding.com/)**, a fast site that works well on a phone, with tap-to-call and a clear "Book your appointment" button.
- **Gave each main style its own page** ([box braids](https://isabraiding.com/services/box-braids/), [cornrows](https://isabraiding.com/services/cornrows/), [twists](https://isabraiding.com/services/twists/), and [goddess braids](https://isabraiding.com/services/goddess-braids/)), so the salon can show up when people search for a style in Savannah.
- **Set up booking without a monthly fee.** The owner didn't want a paid booking tool, so I built a simple booking request form. Each time a client sends one, she gets a message on Telegram right away.
- Set up page titles, descriptions, and structured data that tell Google what the salon does and where it is.

## The last 3 months

From the salon's Google Business Profile, for the 3 months to October 2026:

- **4,723 people viewed the profile.** 87% of them (4,092) found it on Google Search on a phone. Another 312 found it on Google Maps on a phone.
- **975 people took an action:** 450 called, 302 asked for directions, and 223 clicked through to the website.
- **The profile showed up in 1,230 searches.** The top ones:
  1. african hair braiding (402)
  2. african hair braiding savannah ga (324)
  3. african braiding near me (192)
  4. african braiding savannah ga (120)
  5. hair braiding near me (44)

Almost every visit came from a phone. That's why the site and the profile are built for tap-to-call and quick booking first.
