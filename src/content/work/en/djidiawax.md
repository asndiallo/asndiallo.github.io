## The shop

Djidiawax sells afro-contemporary clothing online: sets, jackets and coats, trousers, and tops made with African textiles. The shop runs on WooCommerce.

## What I'm working on

- **Product and category pages:** titles, descriptions, and content so each product and category can show up in Google.
- **Technical SEO and speed:** indexing, structured data, the sitemap, site speed, and WooCommerce fixes.
- **Google Shopping:** listing products in Google Shopping through Merchant Center.
- **Turning visitors into buyers:** product page layout and the checkout.
